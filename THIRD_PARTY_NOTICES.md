# Sources and reuse

## Research paper and supplements

Sorenza P. Bastiaens, Davide Momi, and John D. Griffiths (2025).
*A comprehensive investigation of intracortical and corticothalamic models of the alpha rhythm.*
PLOS Computational Biology 21(4): e1012926.
[Original publication and supporting information](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012926).
Copyright © 2025 Bastiaens et al.

The publisher identifies the article as licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The paper skill preserves the authorship, citation, captions, and source credits.
Retain these credits when reusing extracted text, tables, or figures.

Adaptations in this distribution: PDF text was extracted into Markdown, figures
and dense equations were cropped into linked images, and selected tables were
converted to machine-readable representations. Extraction, formatting, and
navigation were reviewed; material limitations and original source inconsistencies
are documented in the [paper index](skill/bastiaens2025-alpha-models-paper/references/index.md).
This conversion does not imply endorsement of new analyses by the paper's authors.

## Research software

The scientific implementation comes from
[GriffithsLab/Bastiaens2025_AlphaModels](https://github.com/GriffithsLab/Bastiaens2025_AlphaModels),
pinned at commit `74f924beb50f60513ccb7efe065dcc1014d0c953`.
Copyright © 2024 KCNI Whole Brain Modelling Group.

The original [MIT License](mcp/Bastiaens2025_AlphaModels-mcp/repo/Bastiaens2025_AlphaModels/LICENSE)
is included unchanged. The MCP tools bind to that source; they add parameter
validation, input/output handling, and MCP transport. Source files and their
hashes are listed in [SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json](mcp/Bastiaens2025_AlphaModels-mcp/SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json).
Original scientific source files have not been edited. The two excluded
computations are documented in the [usage guide](mcp/Bastiaens2025_AlphaModels-mcp/USAGE.md).
